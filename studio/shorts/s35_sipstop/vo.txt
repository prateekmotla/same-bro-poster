[casual] Market bees percent gira. Sid ki SIP laal. Usne turant stop SIP daba diya. "Pehle market theek ho jaaye, phir dekhenge."
[serious] Galat move. Girte market mein, wahi paanch hazaar ki SIP, zyada units khareedti hai.
COVID wala 2020 yaad kar. Nifty lagbhag adtees percent gira. January mein baarah hazaar teen sau saath, aur teyees March ko, saat hazaar chhe sau das.
November 2020 tak, wapas purane high ke upar. Aur 2021 khatam hote hote, satrah hazaar ke paar.
Jinki SIP chalti rahi, unhone poore fall mein saste units liye. Jinhone roki, woh miss kar gaye.
Fall mein SIP rokna matlab, sale skip karo, aur baad mein full price pe wapas khareedo.
[calm] Rule. SIP paanch saal plus ke liye hoti hai. Fall mein mat roko. Roko sirf tab, jab tera goal ya income badle. Aur haan, past guarantee nahi hai.
Moral. Market ka girna, SIP ka discount season hai.
Ye us dost ko bhejo, jisne pichhli baar market girte hi SIP rok di thi.
