[casual] Sid ka stock das percent upar gaya. Turant bech diya. "Profit is profit, bro!"
Doosra stock tees percent neeche. Usko saalon se pakad ke baitha hai. "Wapas aayega."
[serious] Is trap ka naam hai, disposition effect. Dimaag ko loss book karna bilkul pasand nahi.
Ek study mein das hazaar trading accounts check hue. Winner bechne ke chances, loser se lagbhag saath percent zyada the.
Matlab winners jaldi nikal jaate hain, aur portfolio dheere dheere losers se bhar jaata hai.
Aur mazaa dekh. Jo winners bech diye, unhone agle saal, rakhe hue losers ko, teen point chaar percent se beat kiya.
[calm] Rule. Kharidne se pehle hi exit likh lo. Reason toota toh becho, feeling theek hone pe nahi.
Moral. Losers ko kaato, winners ko chalne do.
Ye us dost ko bhejo, jiske saare long term stocks laal hain.
