[casual] Cafe ke board pe likha hai, free wifi. Sid ne number daala, OTP daala, das second mein connected.
Par bhai, online kuch bhi sach mein free nahi hota. Paise se nahi doge, toh kisi aur tarah se doge.
Free services aksar ads ya data sharing se kamaati hain. Aur tune abhi apna number de diya.
[serious] Asli risk ye hai. CERT-In warn karta hai, public wifi pe unprotected connection ko criminals aaraam se intercept kar sakte hain.
Matlab data chori, paison ka loss, yahan tak ki identity fraud bhi.
Isliye CERT-In bolta hai, public wifi pe na banking, na shopping, na personal info.
[calm] Rule. Payment mobile data pe karo, auto-connect band rakho, aur zaroorat ho toh trusted VPN use karo.
Moral. Agar cheez free hai, toh price shayad tum khud ho.
Ye us dost ko bhejo, jo mall ka har free wifi join kar leta hai.
