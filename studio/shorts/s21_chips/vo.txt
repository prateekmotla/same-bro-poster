[casual] Sid ne bees rupaye ka chips packet khola. Packet itna bada... aur aadha toh hawa hai.
Sab bolte hain, scam hai bhai. Par wo hawa, mostly nitrogen gas hoti hai.
[serious] Oxygen se oil stale ho jaata hai, aur moisture se chips soggy. Nitrogen unko crispy rakhti hai.
Ye gas cushion ka kaam bhi karti hai, taaki chips tum tak crumbs banke na pahunche.
Par hidden part ye hai. Mota packet dekh ke lagta hai zyada khaana hai. Aankhein size dekhti hain, grams nahi.
Is extra space ka naam bhi hai. Slack fill. Freshness bhi, marketing bhi. Dono.
[calm] Rule. Packet ka size bhool jao. Net weight dekho, aur price per gram compare karo.
Moral. Packet ka size nahi, andar ke grams dekho.
Ye us dost ko bhejo, jo bolta hai chips company hawa bechti hai.
