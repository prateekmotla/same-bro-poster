[casual] Ek tareekh. Sid ke phone pe message aata hai. Salary credited. Paintees hazaar.
Sid khush. Is mahine toh savings pakka.
[normal] Bees tareekh. Account mein bache... aath sau rupaye. Aur Sid dost ko message kar raha hai, "bhai, do hazaar milega? salary pe lauta dunga."
Agar ye kahani jaani pehchaani lag rahi hai, toh suno. Paisa ek din mein nahi jaata. Chhote chhote leaks se jaata hai.
Chalo dekhte hain Sid ka paisa gaya kahan.
Pehla week. Salary aayi hai, celebration toh banta hai. Dosto ke saath party, chaar hazaar.
Phir ek naya sneaker. "No cost EMI" tha, toh laga free hai. Teen hazaar mahina, chhe mahine tak.
Doosra week. Office se late aaya, khana kaun banaye. Zomato. Teen sau. Agle din phir. Aur phir.
Raat ko craving. Blinkit. Chips, cold drink, ice cream. Do sau aur.
Teesra week. Teen subscriptions chal rahe hain. Do toh yaad bhi nahi ki kab liye the.
Aur roz ki chai, auto, cold coffee. Har baar bas pachaas, sau rupaye. UPI pe scan kiya, gaya. Pata bhi nahi chala.
[serious] Yahi asli problem hai. Jab cash dete the, haath se paisa jaata dikhta tha. Thoda dard hota tha.
UPI pe na dard hota hai, na dikhta hai. Bas ek tap.
Aur data bhi yahi bolta hai. 2024 ke second half mein, UPI ka average transaction chhota ho gaya. Dukaan pe average payment, sirf chhe sau sattaais rupaye.
Matlab hum bade kharche kam, aur chhote kharche bahut zyada kar rahe hain.
Ab ek simple math dekho. Roz sirf dhaai sau rupaye ke chhote kharche.
Mahine ke saade saat hazaar. Saal ke nabbe hazaar.
Nabbe hazaar. Chai, snacks aur delivery mein. Bina yaad rahe.
Aur EMI ka trap alag. Ek EMI chhoti lagti hai. Lekin teen chaar EMI ek saath, aur salary aane se pehle hi aadhi khatam.
[slower] Problem kamai nahi hai. Problem ye hai ki saving ko hum aakhri mein rakhte hain. Jo bachega, woh save karenge. Aur bachta kabhi kuch nahi.
[calm] Toh fix kya hai? Paanch simple rules.
Ek: pay yourself first. Salary aate hi, pehle saving auto transfer. Phir kharcha.
Do: kharche ke liye alag account. Usme jitna hai, utna hi mahina.
Teen: koi bhi "want" kharidne se pehle, chaubees ghante ruko. Aadhi cheezein tum khud hi bhool jaoge.
Chaar: mahine mein ek baar apna UPI statement dekho. Sach mein. Tumhe khud pe shock lagega.
Aur paanch: EMI sirf zaroorat ke liye. Shauk ke liye nahi.
Ek example dekho. Agar Sid har mahine sirf paanch hazaar, das saal tak invest kare, aur maan lo baarah percent return mile, toh woh ban sakta hai lagbhag saadhe gyaarah lakh.
Chhe lakh usne daala, baaki compounding ne banaya.
Sid ne is mahine pehli baar, salary aate hi paanch hazaar alag kar diye. Bees tareekh ko bhi, account mein paise the.
