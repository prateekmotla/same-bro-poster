# Same Bro — Long 01 "How Live Traders Really Make Money" (~6 min)
Narrator talks TO the viewer; Sid is the example. Delhi Hinglish, rough-casual, no pure Hindi.
Facts:
- SEBI FY25 study: 91% of individual F&O traders lost money; avg loss ₹1,10,069; total ₹1.05 lakh crore.
- SEBI data for FY26 (reported Aug 2026): 43% of individual F&O traders were under 30 (31% in FY22); 89% of them lost money.
- SEBI 2024 rules: SEBI-regulated entities can't associate with / pay unregistered finfluencers giving advice or return claims.
- Feb 2025: SEBI banned a finfluencer + 5 others, impounded ₹53+ crore. Dec 2025: SEBI impounded ₹546 crore from a trading academy for unregistered advisory.
Disclaimer on screen: Example numbers for illustration · not about any specific person · not financial advice.

## VO (Roman Hinglish)

[S1 HOOK — Sid's room, night]
Raat ke saade gyaarah baje. Sid bed pe leta hai, phone pe ek live chal raha hai.
"Bhai log, kal ka setup ekdum ready hai. Premium group join karo, link description mein."
Sid sochta hai... ye banda roz jeetta hai. Main kyun nahi?
Aaj main tumhe dikhaunga ki ye live wale bhai asal mein kamate kaise hain. Aur sach bataun? Trade se jitna kamate hain, usse kahin zyada... tumse kamate hain.

[S2 SID'S STORY — college canteen, metro, room]
Sid ki kahani simple hai. Internship ke pehle paise. Pandrah hazaar.
Canteen mein dost ne phone dikhaya: "Dekh, aaj aath hazaar ka profit."
Usi raat Sid ne account khola. Kiske link se? Usi live wale bhai ke link se.
Pehla hafta? Mast. Do teen trade profit. Screenshot seedha group mein.
Phir ek din, market ulta gaya. Sid ne socha, bas ek trade, recover kar lunga. Lot double.
Woh bhi gaya. Phir dost se paanch hazaar udhaar.
Teen hafte. Pandrah hazaar, zero. Aur ghar pe kisi ko pata bhi nahi.

[S3 DATA — big numbers]
Ab tum bologe, Sid noob tha. Theek hai. Data dekhte hain.
SEBI ki study: FY25 mein, F&O trade karne waale 91% log loss mein the. Average loss? Ek lakh das hazaar.
Aur sabse dangerous baat. FY26 mein F&O traders mein se 43% log tees saal se kam umar ke the. Aur unmein se 89% ne paisa gawaya.
Matlab, das young traders mein se nau. Haar rahe hain.

[S4 THE QUESTION — studio]
Toh sawaal ye hai. Agar nau log haar rahe hain... toh woh live wala bhai roz jeetta kaise dikhta hai?
Kyunki uska asli business trading hai hi nahi. Uske paas paanch income hain. Ek ek karke dekhte hain.

[S5 INCOME 1 — referral]
Pehli: referral. "Mere link se account kholo."
Maan lo uske ek lakh subscribers hain. Das hazaar ne link se account khola. Sirf hazaar log roz trade karte hain, das order, bees rupaye brokerage.
Roz ka do lakh brokerage. Usme se dus percent bhi mila, toh bees hazaar roz. Mahine ke chaar lakh.

[S6 INCOME 2 — premium group]
Doosri: premium Telegram group. Nau sau ninyanve rupaye mahina.
Sirf do hazaar log bhi join karein, toh bees lakh mahina.
Aur calls galat gayi? Koi refund nahi. "Market ka risk hai bhai."

[S7 INCOME 3 — course]
Teesri: course aur mentorship. "Mere jaisa trader bano." Pandrah hazaar ka course.
Ek batch mein paanch sau students. Pachattar lakh. Ek batch se.

[S8 INCOME 4 — ads & sponsors]
Chauthi: ads, sponsors, super chats. Jitne zyada views, utna paisa. Aur views kahan se aate hain? Tumhari ummeed se.

[S9 INCOME 5 — screenshots]

[S10 THE TWIST]
Ab dhyaan se dekho. In paanch mein se ek bhi income, trade jeetne pe depend nahi karti.
Woh jeete ya haare, uska paisa aata rahega. Bas ek cheez chahiye. Tum. Roz. Trade karte raho.

[S11 SEBI ACTION]
Aur ye sirf meri theory nahi hai.
2024 mein SEBI ne rule laaya: registered brokers aur companies, aise unregistered finfluencers ko paise nahi de sakte.
Feb 2025 mein SEBI ne ek finfluencer aur paanch logon ko ban kiya, aur tirpan crore se zyada impound kiye.
Aur December 2025 mein, ek trading academy se paanch sau chhiyalis crore impound hue. Unregistered advice dene ke liye.
